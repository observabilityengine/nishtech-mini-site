01 — WHAT IT DOES

Three things, on one computer in your office.

The agent asks first

Every action your agent wants to take comes to VIL Mini before it runs. Nothing skips the ask.

A named person approves

Approvals belong to a person you named, not to a password passed around. Your approver decides with their own PIN, and the record names them.

The record shows everything

Every request, every answer, and every outcome goes into one record. If someone changes an entry later, the record check shows it. You can run that check yourself.

What it does not do

VIL Mini does not decide what your agent may do. You decide, and your approver decides. VIL Mini checks the approval and keeps the record. It covers the actions sent through it on the one computer where it runs. A fresh install refuses any action that was never set up.

05 — QUESTIONS

Questions buyers ask.

Does VIL Mini run the actions itself?

No. It checks the approval, then hands the action to the system you connected to carry it out. That system is yours.

What happens when an action has no approval?

It does not run. Actions that need a person wait until an approver says yes or no. The wait and the answer both go into the record.

What does the record prove?

Every request, answer, and outcome is written into one record, and each entry links to the entry before it. Change an entry and the record check fails. You can run that check yourself, with the program running or on a copy of the file with the program stopped.

Where do my files live?

On your computer. Nishtech does not host your install, your record stays with you, and your keys are made on your machine the first time the program starts.

How long does one payment cover?

Each monthly payment covers a 30 day licence. When it runs out, renew and put the new licence file in place of the old one. Nothing else changes.
